The Evolve System

How Evolve Protects the People You Care About

Three layers of intelligent technology working together — so nothing gets missed and no one feels alone.

4D Radar Detection

Vayyar Care sensors use 4D imaging radar to monitor an entire room — including the bathroom where 80% of falls occur — 24 hours a day. No cameras. No wearables. Complete privacy protection at all times.

24/7 Remote Nurse Support

The Evolve monitoring center is staffed around the clock by trained wellness professionals. When a fall is detected, a real person responds — coordinating care, dispatching emergency services, and notifying family.

Nobi Smart Lighting

AI-powered ceiling lights that blend seamlessly into any room — brightening paths at night to prevent falls before they happen, while quietly detecting events and alerting caregivers when help is needed.

Vayyar Technology

Radar Waves — Never Video

The Vayyar sensor detects movement and position using radar signals — the same technology in modern vehicles and aircraft. It sees through walls and in complete darkness, but captures zero images or video.

100% Privacy · No Cameras · No Images
Nobi Technology

Digital Stick Figure — Never Video

Nobi Smart Lights convert movement into an anonymous digital stick figure for analysis. Your loved one's face, identity, and appearance are never captured — just the motion data needed to detect a fall.

100% Privacy · Anonymous Detection · No Recording
Technology

Built Different. Because It Had to Be.

Works in Complete Darkness

Radar technology requires no light source — monitoring continues reliably through the night, in steam-filled bathrooms, and in every condition that cameras and motion sensors fail.

Privacy by Design

No images or video are ever captured. Radar sensors track position and movement without identifying the person — protecting dignity while ensuring complete safety coverage.

Proactive Health Insights

Evolve tracks movement patterns, bathroom visit frequency, time at rest, and activity levels — detecting subtle changes before they become emergencies so caregivers can intervene early.

Concierge-Style Daily Support

From medication reminders to arranging transportation and following up after incidents — the Evolve support team handles the daily details so families and seniors don't have to.

What Evolve Replaces

Traditional fall alert systems put the burden on the person who just fell. Evolve eliminates the need for:

  • Wearable devices that go unworn
  • Emergency buttons out of reach during a fall
  • Privacy-invasive cameras in personal spaces
  • Alert cords and pull strings
  • Unreliable floor mats and pressure sensors

The result

True passive protection — always on, always aware, never in the way.
