Homes weren't built for growing older

Narrow doorways that won't fit a walker. Staircases that become a daily risk. Tubs and low toilets that make simple tasks dangerous. For many families, the home someone has lived in for decades slowly becomes the biggest threat to their safety — and assisted living isn't always the answer they want, or can afford. A fall serious enough to need hospitalization can cost more, and take more independence, than fixing the home ever would.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a home safety assessment?

An in-home evaluation performed by a physical or occupational therapist to identify ways to make your home safer and easier to navigate as you age.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

No. This is direct access, private pay — no physician referral required.

Is this covered by insurance?

The therapy evaluation may be billable in some cases, but modifications and renovations are private pay.

How much does a project cost?

The assessment itself is a flat $299. Modifications depend on your needs, materials, and home construction — we quote based on your actual situation after the evaluation.

How long does a project take?

A single grab bar can be done quickly; larger projects like ramps or bathroom conversions may take two weeks to two months.

Do you serve areas outside Tulsa?

Yes, generally within 50 miles of the greater Tulsa metro area.

Who actually does the evaluation?

A licensed physical or occupational therapist from our clinical team — the same clinicians who provide home health therapy visits.

Who does the actual construction work?

Our contractor partner, Grab Bars of Tulsa, handles installation. We stay involved to make sure the finished work matches the clinical recommendations.

What if I just want one thing fixed, like a grab bar?

That's fine — not every visit turns into a full renovation. Many families start with one or two specific fixes and add more later.

Can this work alongside fall monitoring or a Nobi/Vayyar system?

Yes. Many families pair a home safety assessment with our fall detection and remote monitoring services for a more complete safety plan.

Is the $299 assessment fee applied toward the renovation cost?

This is still being finalized — check with our team when you schedule, as this policy may vary.
